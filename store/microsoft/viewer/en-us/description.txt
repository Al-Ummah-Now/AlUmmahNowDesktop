Al Ummah Now is an Islamic video and community platform. Creators upload videos, go live and post to their channels, and the community watches and shares, in Arabic and English.

With Al Ummah Now for Windows you can:
• Explore the latest and most watched videos, and browse by category: Qur'an recitation, Tafsir, Hadith studies, Fiqh, Islamic history, Nasheeds and Ramadan
• Watch with subtitles, chapters, playback speed and quality choices
• Control playback with your keyboard's media keys and the Windows media controls
• Sign in to your Al Ummah Now account
• Use the app in Arabic or English, with a light or dark theme
